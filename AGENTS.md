# Cable clip recovery

For cable insertion simulations, check each wire's actual retained position after the gripper releases it. If a wire remains outside a clip, attempt to grasp that wire from the opposite longitudinal side of the clip, favoring the section that protrudes farther, and drag it into the clip. Stop the pull when the wire is retained, release, and check again. Recheck the other wires and previously filled clips because a recovery pull can disturb them.

Make recovery conditional on the simulated wire positions. Record attempted grasps, sides, retention results, and any exhausted retry limits. A commanded insertion pose alone is not evidence that the wire entered the clip. Preserve and report unsuccessful outcomes.
